Our Story

A Venue Built for Your Most Cherished Moments

Ponderosa Events Place is Binangonan's premier upscale event destination — where elegance meets warmth. Nestled along Manila East Road in Tagpos, our venue was designed to transform every celebration into an unforgettable experience.

Whether you're planning an intimate wedding, a grand debut, a milestone birthday, or a professional corporate gathering, our dedicated team and beautifully appointed spaces ensure every detail is handled with care and precision.
